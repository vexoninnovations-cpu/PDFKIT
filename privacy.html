Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

Welcome to PDFKIT (`pdfkittool.in`). Your privacy is critically important to us.

1. Local Processing of Files

All PDF tools available on PDFKIT (such as merging, splitting, and compressing) run entirely client-side inside your web browser using JavaScript and web technologies. Your files are never uploaded, stored, or viewed on our remote servers. Everything happens locally on your device.

2. Log Files and Analytics

Like most standard websites, we use web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics and Google Search Console) to understand how visitors interact with our site. This may include standard information like your IP address, browser type, referring pages, and timestamps. This data helps us improve user experience and troubleshoot technical issues.

3. Cookies and Advertisements

We may use third-party vendors, such as Google AdSense, to serve ads when you visit our website. Google uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other websites across the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please feel free to reach out to us through our website.
